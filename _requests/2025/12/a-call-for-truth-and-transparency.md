To our brothers and sisters in the Lord serving in the Provincial Office, the College of Bishops, the Executive Committee, and the Provincial Council of the Anglican Church of North America,

We write to you as those who dearly love the holy Church of God and the riches of faith we have inherited in our Anglican tradition. We also write to you grieving that the Church’s visible unity and holiness is besmirched by crisis, scandal, and abuse.

Out of both love and grief, **we request an independent, third party assessment of the actions of the Provincial Office, Provincial Response Team, Court for the Trial of a Bishop, Provincial Tribunal, present and former Archbishops, and the College of Bishops, with regards to the investigation and trial of Bishop Stewart Ruch**. We make this request not in response to the specific verdict reached but due to the many serious concerns raised both publicly and privately at each stage of the process. We are troubled by, for example: [the resignation of three members of the PRT](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://religionnews.com/2022/01/19/three-members-of-acna-response-team-resign-from-overseeing-abuse-investigation/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665849722&usg=AOvVaw0wZD6MlgY9kY76VNSgn--x) in 2022, [the public dispute between Archbishop Beach and Bishop Ruch](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://web.archive.org/web/20230818001328/https://anglicanchurch.net/update-on-the-diocese-of-the-upper-midwest-june-7-2023/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665849918&usg=AOvVaw2NNwczWqy1fVgz9VksZV3S) in 2023, and [the resignation of Provincial Prosecutor C. Alan Runyan](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://anglicanchurch.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/7-19-25-Provincial-Prosecutor-Resignation-Letter-to-Archbishop-Woods.pdf&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665850078&usg=AOvVaw3uUndCmttaqbXajIYRMjvx) and [Deputy Prosecutor Rachel Thebeau](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.christianpost.com/news/prosecutor-in-acna-bishop-abuse-case-alleges-misconduct-in-trial.html&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665850194&usg=AOvVaw0rozOvoTNQKX_y8bZss90J) earlier this year. Each of these events on their own would be cause for Provincial self-examination and repentance. Combined with recent public news coverage, [allegations against Archbishop Wood](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.washingtonpost.com/investigations/2025/11/07/anglican-church-archbishop-harassment/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665850431&usg=AOvVaw1I1uGhqVPzCEtDUB-pYoDj) and [discrepancies between provincial communications and the accounts of credible witnesses](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://anglican.ink/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/0-Final-Cover-letter-for-presentment-2.0.pdf&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665850616&usg=AOvVaw0mWnnFw7yIq-8civlENkg1), they mandate truth-telling and change. For the good of our Province and the integrity of our witness, and in line with a [similar request from the Standing Committee of the Anglican Diocese of South Carolina](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://mailchi.mp/9692eb7e0ad3/adosc-standing-committee-re-the-ruch-trial&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665850871&usg=AOvVaw0a7Gd1z_yrX7XerThLUNoR) and the [College of Bishops’ own expressed commitment to repentance and rebuilding trust](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://anglicanchurch.net/report-from-the-college-of-bishops-meeting-in-plano-tx/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665851025&usg=AOvVaw1oV7nzWF3cCjYyIICRrYnO), we believe an independent assessment is both necessary and urgent for the ACNA to “bear fruit in keeping with repentance.”

We were glad to hear during the Provincial Office Hours on December 12th that the College of Bishops is open to hiring a third party to conduct an assessment. **In order for such an assessment to repair rather than compound the “[significant deficit of trust toward the College of Bishops](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://anglicanchurch.net/report-from-the-college-of-bishops-meeting-in-plano-tx/&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665851584&usg=AOvVaw0gMGjfVzXLHjpB59yGSYrQ),” we request the following:**  


1. We ask that the assessment be broad in scope so that multiple systems, individuals, and other factors that may have contributed to what occurred may be examined: such as ACNA’s canons, distribution of power, and institutional culture.
2. We ask that the assessment be conducted by an organization or individual that possesses significant expertise as an investigative and compliance firm (rather than a law firm), a public record of prioritizing truth and care for victims over institutional protection, and significant experience conducting trauma-informed assessments and investigations in religious spaces.
3. We ask that the investigating party be fully independent, not bound by attorney-client privilege, and free from personal ties to any Bishop or employee of the Province. We suggest that the investigating party be selected by a panel consisting of victim advocates, members of the laity, and male and female clergy, who are in good standing and in agreement with the [Fundamental Declarations of the Province](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://anglicanchurch.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ACNA-Constitution-and-Canons_2025.pdf&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1766184665852830&usg=AOvVaw0ZVSwIDLsjtFjn3Yxq_UlJ), and who are in turn selected by the Executive Committee, rather than by the College of Bishops or the Provincial Office.
4. We ask that the scope of the investigation and contract with the investigating party be made public with a three week period for comment and revision prior to being finalized.
5. We ask that the Provincial Office and the College of Bishops publicly commit to full cooperation with the investigating party, trusting the words of our Lord that “the truth will set you free.”
6. We ask that the Provincial Office and the College of Bishops commit to releasing the final report of the investigating party publicly and promptly regardless of the findings or outcome. We ask that the final report be unredacted except as needed to protect victims.

We are aware that an assessment such as we request raises practical questions and anxiety about what might be revealed. We ask you to remember alongside us that, in the words of our Lord, “there is nothing hidden that will not be disclosed, and nothing concealed that will not be known or brought out into the open.” We pray for ourselves and for our Province, that we might be more afraid of sin and error kept hidden than of sin and error revealed. For, “if we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us. If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”

**Come, brothers and sisters – let us walk in the light of the Lord!**

In faith, hope, and love,

Anglicans for Truth, Renewal, and Accountability
