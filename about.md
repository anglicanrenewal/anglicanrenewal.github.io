Anglicans for Truth, Renewal and Accountability (ATRA) is an informal, grassroots group of
clergy and laity from multiple dioceses in the Anglican Church of North America (ACNA)
actively working together on concrete steps toward accountability, renewal, and a healthier
culture within the ACNA.

Currently, laypeople and clergy from 17 ACNA dioceses have connected with ATRA, expressing
that they share our mission, vision, and concerns.

## Why are we here?

Anglicans for Truth, Renewal and Accountability exists to serve the ACNA by working towards healthy systems and institutional culture across the Province through:

- Gathering together clergy and laity in the ACNA who share our values, concerns, and goals;
- Informing those who connect with us about ACNA governance, decision-making, and happenings;
- Advocating for concrete steps towards institutional health, such as increased transparency, strengthened checks and balances, and measures that protect the vulnerable and hold leaders accountable.

We are here because we have a vision of what a healthy ACNA might look like, and we want to work and pray towards that end:

- We envision an ACNA in which decision-making, representation, and responsibility are appropriately shared among bishops, clergy, and laity.
- We envision an ACNA in which transparent decision-making is practiced at every level of governance.
- We envision an ACNA in which both canons and culture protect the vulnerable and hold decision-makers accountable.
- We envision an ACNA marked by charity, mutual respect, and unity in the Spirit that makes space for a range of faithful perspectives and convictions.

We love the ACNA, and we desire to see this expression of the Church healthy for the sake of our Lord’s own mission to heal the world.
